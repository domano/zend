How to Play

  • Click/Drag to place particles
  • Touch/Drag on mobile devices
  • Select materials: Sand, Water, Fire, Acid
  • Use 1-4 keys to switch materials quickly
  • Materials react: Water+Fire=Steam, Acid+Sand=Gas, Fire+Sand=Extinguish
  • Press R key or click Reset to clear all particles
  • Works in real-time with other players!
