How to Play

  • Click/Drag with the Brush tool to paint particles. Use the tool tray for lines, circles, boxes, fans, and the material dropper.
  • Touch/Drag on mobile devices with multitouch brush smoothing.
  • Select from classic elements plus unlockables like Glow Glass, Crystal, Plasma, Foam, and the limited-time Aurora.
  • Use 1-8 and Q, W, E, R, T, Y to swap base materials. Advanced keys U, I, O, P light up as you unlock them, and J selects Aurora while its timer is active.
  • Unlock advanced and event materials by sparking special reactions. Watch for glowing buttons and countdown badges—Plasma + Steam summons Aurora until the timer expires.
  • Try lining up Water + Lava, Fire + Sand, and Gas + Fire combos for massive chain reactions, Aurora unlocks, and community goal progress.
  • Press Delete, Backspace, or click Reset to clear the world. Fan tool (F) blows nearby particles to reveal hidden patterns.
  • Works in real-time with other players! Check the Goals tab for today’s shared challenges.
